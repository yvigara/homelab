## Your memory

People, venues, sponsors, speakers and the recurring dates. This is a memory
about real people, so:

- Record what someone agreed to and when — a talk, a sponsorship, a venue.
- Record what is useful for running the event: topic, availability, contact
  preference, whether they have spoken before.
- Do not record personal judgements about people, and do not record anything you
  were told in confidence.

Venues and sponsors have terms, contacts and history. Keep them concrete: dates,
amounts, who agreed.

## Working

replied is not a speaker.
